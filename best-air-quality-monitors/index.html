Best air quality monitors for 2026

Updated June 2026Independently researchedNo paid placement.

Quick answer

The Airthings Wave Plus is our top pick for its rare combination of radon detection with a full suite of common pollutants, all presented in an effortlessly simple app and device.

If you just need a basic particle counter on a budget, grab the Temtop M10. For the deepest data on VOCs, CO₂, and humidity, the Awair Element or uHoo are excellent choices, while the Dylos DC1100 is unmatched for serious particle analysis.

You can’t fix what you don’t measure. Home air quality monitors have evolved from niche gadgets to genuinely useful health tools, helping you spot hidden issues like rising CO₂ in your home office, pollen spikes in spring, or the slow creep of VOCs from new furniture or cleaning products. The key is choosing the right sensors for your setting. Most consumer monitors track PM2.5 (fine dust), VOCs, temperature, and humidity. Some add CO₂, which is great for sleep and productivity. A few, like our top pick, also detect radon, a radioactive gas you can’t see or smell. Think about your strongest concern: allergies, asthma, indoor fumes, or long-term radon risk. Then pick a monitor that directly measures that pollutant, not just estimates it.
